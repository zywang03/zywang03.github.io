<h2 id="about-me">About Me</h2>

<p>Hello, I am <span class="date-tag">Zhongyi Wang</span>, a master student pursuing an M.Eng. in Computer Science and Engineering (2025.09 - present, expected 2028.06) at <a href="https://www.shanghaitech.edu.cn/eng/">ShanghaiTech University</a>, advised by <a href="https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/faculty/wangjingya/">Prof. Jingya Wang</a>, jointly with <a href="https://shiye21.github.io/">Prof. Ye Shi</a> in Shanghai, China.
